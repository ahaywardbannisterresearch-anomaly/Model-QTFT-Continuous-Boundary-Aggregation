## Mandatory FACA preflight — all QTFT mathematical work

Before any new QTFT modelling, extension, proof, or mathematical/physical interpretation, consult FACA and record a brief **FACA preflight disposition** in the relevant model/test plan or research note: identify assumed state spaces, labels, roots, relations, directions, operators, transition schedules, ordering, probabilities and realisation mechanisms; audit circularity, symmetry and labelling invariance, hidden physical-time or dynamics assumptions; preserve simpler/nontriadic/null controls, falsification pathways, prior-work attribution, negative evidence and distinctions between conditional mathematical findings and physical claims. This is a prospective research gate; do not rewrite frozen historical results to retrofit it.

**Mandatory expansion trigger:** When a novel premise, counterexample, mathematical technique, model discrepancy, missing dependency, potential physical bridge or observational limit exposes inadequate FACA coverage, mark **FACA EXPANSION CANDIDATE** with gap, affected work, significance and proposed test. Flag consequential blockers directly to Andrew in chat; otherwise raise them in the existing QTFT Fortnightly Review. Seek Andrew's review before changing the authoritative audit; log accepted/rejected/deferred proposals, avoid duplicates and verify subsequent FACA/AIX writes.
