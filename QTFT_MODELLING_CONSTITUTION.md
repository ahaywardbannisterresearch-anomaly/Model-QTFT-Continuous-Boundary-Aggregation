# QTFT Modelling Constitution

Canonical guardrail for QTFT coded work.

## Source of truth
Preprint 1 and subsequently locked QTFT architecture decisions control the ontology. Mathematical convenience and familiar physics do not outrank the QTFT framework.

## Canonical architecture
- Dᵢ: universally available divergent dynamic.
- Cₒ: universally available convergent dynamic; not memory and not simply negative Dᵢ.
- Tₑ: universally available ordering/admissibility dynamic; not time and not a passive one-way gate.
- Iₙ: deeper informational/relational constraint domain; not a fourth field and never an undefined hidden fix.
- AIR: jointly generated realised/admissible interaction regime in which Dᵢ, Cₒ and Tₑ co-act within Iₙ; not a fourth component, Tₑ, Iₙ, memory, D∩C∩T, or D*C*T by definition.
- Reciprocity: Dᵢ, Cₒ and Tₑ may mutually reshape one another.
- Continual collapse/evolution: recursive whole-triad realisation process; not synonymous with Cₒ convergence.
- RC: Realisation Configuration; do not insert into RC phenomena QTFT is trying to derive.
- SSI: Symmetry State Identifier Ω△=(r_D,r_C,r_T); composition descriptor, not AIR and not by itself a complete RC.

## Do not bake in the answer
Encode QTFT foundational assumptions, not desired downstream outcomes. Do not begin with white-hole, black-hole, gravity, GR, expansion, time, boundary-motion, locality, conservation, epoch or other target mathematics and then layer QTFT terminology over it. Develop the QTFT primary model independently, then use established mathematics as a connection, reconstruction or comparison test.

## Departure rule
Any abstraction that changes/removes canonical QTFT architecture must be labelled:

> **DEPARTURE / CONTROL MODEL — NOT THE QTFT PRIMARY MODEL**

This includes symmetric interchangeable channels, generic state machines, diffusion/advection imposed as primitives, external potentials, fixed centres, lookup systems, or imported equations that replace QTFT dynamics.

## Preserve departures
Maintain a Departure / Control Pathway Register: what changed, why tested, result, why not promoted, and when it may become useful. Controls are not disposable; they may become valuable if QTFT fails/diverges or as alternative bridges.

## Mandatory pre-flight
Before new models/stages/equation families, explicitly state: Dᵢ role; Cₒ role; Tₑ role; Iₙ role; AIR generation; reciprocal coupling; continual-realisation scope; theory assumptions vs predicted outcomes; imported mathematics status; falsifier.

## Equation explanation rule
Before freezing an equation record: formal equation; every symbol; plain-English meaning; QTFT role; assumptions; risks; matched control/alternative; falsifier/rejection condition.

## Representation vs theory
A failed representation does not automatically falsify the QTFT concept. Audit whether the representation actually contained the relevant QTFT dynamics before interpreting a negative result. Toy success likewise does not establish physical QTFT without strong controls.

## Fair controls
Do not cripple nulls. Preserve and report controls that outperform QTFT.

## No goalpost movement
Freeze hypotheses/nulls, equations/ranges, conditions, controls, diagnostics and decision criteria before execution. Scientific changes after execution require a new version/stage; mechanical fixes must be logged and authorised.

## Coded-work workflow
**Constitution/architecture check → design → plain-English review → preregistration/implementation plan → checkpoint → execution → validation → interpretation → checkpoint/push.**

Record source SHA, outputs, expected/executed/pass counts where applicable, result hashes where used, and repository validation. Do not auto-commit scientific work unless explicitly authorised.

## External maths classification
Explicitly classify imported work as: Primary QTFT derivation; Connection test; Reconstruction test; Control/comparator; or Departure pathway.

## Explain before freeze
Technical sophistication is not a substitute for conceptual clarity. Explain complicated machinery in ordinary language and why it is needed before freezing it.

## Stop rule
If implementation quietly changes ontology, imports a desired result, hides a constraint, introduces unjustified preferred structure, or cannot be explained against this constitution: **STOP** and correct or relabel it.

## Canonical main / branch-presence rule
The canonical constitution belongs on `main`. Every active QTFT modelling or coded-work branch must also contain the current canonical constitution and verify that it matches `main` exactly before scientific/coded execution.

Pre-flight must confirm:
1. `QTFT_MODELLING_CONSTITUTION.md` exists on the current branch;
2. it is content/hash-equivalent to the canonical `main` copy;
3. the check is recorded in the stage/notebook/method log.

If absent or stale: **STOP** and sync from `main` before continuing. New branches must inherit/sync the constitution at creation; existing long-lived branches must sync it before their next scientific execution. Branch-specific notes may extend but not silently weaken the canonical rules.

### Required stage header
> **QTFT Constitution check:** completed. Canonical constitution present on current branch and verified against `main`. Primary model conforms / departure explicitly labelled. Desired downstream outcomes are not encoded. Controls and falsifiers are identified.
