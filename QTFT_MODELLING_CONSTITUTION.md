# QTFT Modelling Constitution

Canonical guardrail for QTFT coded work.

Preprint 1 and subsequently locked QTFT architecture decisions control the ontology. Dᵢ is the universally available divergent dynamic; Cₒ the universally available convergent dynamic (not memory or simply negative Dᵢ); Tₑ the universally available ordering/admissibility dynamic (not time or a passive one-way gate); Iₙ the deeper informational/relational constraint domain (not a fourth field or hidden fix); AIR the jointly generated realised/admissible Dᵢ/Cₒ/Tₑ interaction regime within Iₙ. Reciprocal D/C/T influence is canonical unless deliberately removed as a labelled control. Continual collapse/evolution is recursive whole-triad realisation, not Cₒ convergence.

## Do not bake in the answer
Encode QTFT foundations, not desired downstream outcomes. Do not start with white-hole/black-hole, gravity/GR, expansion, time, boundary motion, epochs, locality, conservation or other target mathematics and then layer QTFT terminology over it. Test external mathematics later as explicit connection, reconstruction, comparator or departure pathways.

## Departure rule
Any abstraction changing/removing canonical QTFT architecture must be labelled:
> **DEPARTURE / CONTROL MODEL — NOT THE QTFT PRIMARY MODEL**
Preserve departures in a pathway register; they may become valuable if the primary QTFT route fails/diverges.

## Mandatory pre-flight
Before major coded/model work state Dᵢ/Cₒ/Tₑ/Iₙ/AIR roles, reciprocity, continual-realisation scope, assumptions vs predictions, imported-maths status and falsifier.

## Equation explanation
Before freezing equations record formal form, symbols, plain-English meaning, QTFT role, assumptions, risks, controls/alternatives and rejection condition.

## Representation versus theory
A failed representation does not automatically falsify QTFT if it omitted relevant dynamics. Audit fidelity before interpretation. Positive toy results also require fair controls.

## Workflow
**Constitution/architecture check → design → plain-English review → preregistration/plan → checkpoint → execution → validation → interpretation → checkpoint/push.**
Freeze choices before execution, log authorised fixes, preserve fair controls, and do not auto-commit scientific work without explicit authorisation.

### Required stage header
> **QTFT Constitution check:** completed. Primary model conforms / departure explicitly labelled. Desired downstream outcomes are not encoded. Controls and falsifiers are identified.
